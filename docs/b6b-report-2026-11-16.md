## What "need met without a pipe" means here

One unpiped call in agent mode (`CLAUDECODE=1`, no tty, merged stream), on a lab fixture for the need. It is not a replay of the original call. A transcript holds no output text, so the original cannot be replayed.

- `result`: the outcome shows in the last 3 lines and the output has at most 45 lines.
- `error_line`: the error text is in the output, or `ladder log <path> --head 30` reads the log, and the output has at most 45 lines.
- `full_log`: the output names a log path, `ladder log` reads it, and the output has at most 45 lines.
- `list`, `doctor`, `help` slots: the output has at most 45 lines (doctor with need `result` also needs a non-empty last line).
- Loose rule: drops the 45 line limit. B1 shows 36 on the loose rule and 10 on the strict rule: B1 puts the summary and log path in the output, but still prints all 300 lines.

## Limits, said plainly

- The 36 of 36 for B5 is partly by design: the probes are the same lab tasks that B3a and B3b were built to pass. It shows the capped output and `ladder log` meet the three needs on those fixtures. It does not show that every original call would be met.
- The `list`, `doctor` and `help` probes only test the line limit. All three builds pass them, so they add 10 to every build.
- The `doctor` probe runs with a fake offline `gh`, so it exits 1. The probe does not use the exit code.
- Fixtures are per need and sub group, not per `fixture_id` (18 ids). One probe serves many ids.
- The sample is Claude only. The need label comes from the pipe shape, not from the user's intent (README of the export).
- The live agent questions did not run (they bill the API).
